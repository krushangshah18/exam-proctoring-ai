# **Brand and Design Guidelines**

## **1\. Purpose**

This document defines the visual identity, tone, communication style, and UX principles of the AI-Based Online Exam Proctoring System.

---

## **2\. Brand Personality**

### **2.1 Core Traits**

* Professional  
* Trustworthy  
* Calm  
* Neutral  
* Supportive  
* Transparent

### **2.2 Emotional Positioning**

The product must feel:

* Non-threatening  
* Fair  
* Respectful  
* Serious  
* Reliable

The system must never feel punitive or aggressive.

---

## **3\. Tone of Voice**

### **3.1 General Tone**

* Clear  
* Formal but friendly  
* Neutral  
* Reassuring

### **3.2 Message Guidelines**

#### **Warnings**

Use calm language.

Example:  
"Warning: Please keep your face visible to continue the exam."

Avoid:  
"Cheating detected."

#### **Errors**

Explain reason and solution.

Example:  
"Camera access is required. Please enable permissions."

#### **Admin Messages**

Professional and factual.

Example:  
"Multiple face detected at 14:32."

---

## **4\. Color Palette**

### **4.1 Primary Colors**

* Primary Blue: \#2563EB (Trust, stability)  
* Dark Blue: \#1E3A8A  
* White: \#FFFFFF

### **4.2 Secondary Colors**

* Gray Light: \#F3F4F6  
* Gray Dark: \#374151

### **4.3 Status Colors**

* Success (Green): \#16A34A  
* Warning (Amber): \#F59E0B  
* Error (Red): \#DC2626  
* Info (Blue): \#3B82F6

### **4.4 Usage Rules**

* Do not overuse red  
* Use neutral backgrounds  
* Maintain high contrast

---

## **5\. Typography**

### **5.1 Primary Font**

* Inter / Roboto / System UI

### **5.2 Hierarchy**

* Headings: Bold  
* Subheadings: Medium  
* Body: Regular  
* Metadata: Small

### **5.3 Accessibility**

* Minimum font size: 14px  
* Line height: 1.5+

---

## **6\. Layout Principles**

### **6.1 Grid System**

* 12-column responsive grid  
* Mobile-first approach

### **6.2 Spacing**

* Use 8px spacing system  
* Avoid clutter

### **6.3 Alignment**

* Left-aligned content  
* Centered primary actions

---

## **7\. Component Design Guidelines**

### **7.1 Buttons**

Primary:

* Blue background  
* White text

Secondary:

* White background  
* Blue border

Danger:

* Red background

### **7.2 Forms**

* Clear labels  
* Inline validation  
* Helper text

### **7.3 Modals**

* Neutral background  
* Clear actions  
* Easy dismiss

### **7.4 Tables**

* Zebra rows  
* Sticky headers  
* Sortable columns

---

## **8\. Status Indicators**

### **8.1 Student Status**

* OK: Green  
* Warning: Amber  
* Flagged: Red  
* Disconnected: Gray

### **8.2 Risk Meter**

* Progress bar  
* Color-coded  
* Numeric value

---

## **9\. UX Principles**

### **9.1 Student UX**

* Minimize stress  
* Reduce distractions  
* Clear feedback  
* Predictable behavior

### **9.2 Admin UX**

* Information density  
* Fast navigation  
* Clear filtering  
* Drill-down support

### **9.3 System Admin UX**

* Data-focused  
* Log-centric  
* High visibility

---

## **10\. Accessibility Guidelines**

* WCAG 2.1 AA compliance  
* Keyboard navigation  
* Screen reader support  
* Color-blind friendly palettes

---

## **11\. Do’s and Don’ts**

### **11.1 Do**

* Use consistent terminology  
* Provide confirmations  
* Show progress  
* Log all critical actions  
* Display timestamps

### **11.2 Don’t**

* Use threatening language  
* Hide important info  
* Auto-terminate without explanation  
* Overload UI  
* Use dark patterns

---

## **12\. Iconography**

* Use simple line icons  
* Avoid decorative icons  
* Consistent stroke width

Recommended: Lucide / Heroicons

---

## **13\. Illustrations & Media**

* Minimal  
* Neutral themes  
* No distracting animations  
* No gamification

---

## **14\. Notification System**

### **14.1 Types**

* Info  
* Warning  
* Error  
* Success

### **14.2 Behavior**

* Auto-dismiss info  
* Persistent warnings  
* Logged errors

---

## **15\. Branding Assets**

### **15.1 Logo**

* No hidden penalties
